# Portfolio illustrations

Home, Stories, Projects and Contact use the same approved Wwwzard artwork and the public `@posecraft/runtime` package. The portfolio owns a JSON export for each placement. No page imports Posecraft source files, authoring factories or Studio.

| Scene | Authored behavior | Website events |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Type, use the trackpad, scroll, sigh, react to laptop closures, accumulate anger and recover through breathing | `laptop-click`, `gust-left`, `gust-right`, `visitor`; explicit `close-laptop` / `open-laptop` remain available |
| Stories | Read, pause, think, visibly turn a page and acknowledge a visitor | `turn-page` from a book click; `visitor` |
| Projects | Draw a car plan, lift the pencil, inspect the model and pause | `inspect-project` from a model click; `visitor` |
| Contact | Type, cover the face on errors, prepare and throw a plane, close the laptop and nap | `ready`, `typing`, `almost-done`, `sending`, `sent`, `error`, `wake`, `visitor` |

Home laptop clicks interrupt the current action and move the lid independently while Wwwzard pulls his hands clear into a puzzled reaction, then reopens it himself. Each closure adds 28 anger points, bounded at 100. At 65 or more, Wwwzard reacts angrily and performs two breathing exercises. Each exhale lowers anger; recovery clears the remainder. Anger stays internal; visitors see his reactions, without a gauge. Manual close/open commands retain their separate semantics. Pointer speed and direction near the plant drive anchored leaf deformation through shared `hover-fast` bindings with an editable radius.

Stories begins its first turn after 2.2 seconds; Projects begins inspecting after 2.6 seconds. Clicking their props requests those actions directly. Enter or Space on a focused illustration provides a keyboard equivalent. Contact uses these keys to wake up.

The Contact adapter receives activity names, a count of completed required fields, and bounded hand offsets from a physical QWERTY key map. Typed text is never passed to or recorded by the runtime. Q/A/Z move the left hand; Backspace moves and holds the right. Brief taps have a 100 ms minimum presentation, key repeat holds still, and each hand releases independently. Blur, submission, hidden tabs and disposal clear pending key work. Software keyboards, IME, paste and speech input use short alternating taps, with deletions assigned to the right hand. Error, send and sleep clips retain priority over keyboard channels. While holding the plane, all keys use the free anatomical right hand, selected by saved clip-filtered motion layers from bounded `keySolo` offsets; the left-hand grip remains fixed. Focus does not start typing. Input starts typing within 120 ms; 450 ms of inactivity or blur clears the typing signal. Three of four filled required fields signal `almost-done`. A casement swings open with a gust that bends the leaves and rocks his torso, head and hat, while he picks up a plane. His bent elbow supports a finger pinch at the paper fold; the plane stays behind his fingers during the hold. Native validation triggers the face-covering reaction; corrections interrupt it. A failed submission lets the thrown plane finish its flight, then retrieves another plane and keeps the window open for retry. Send starts a release through the open aperture. The plane shrinks and fades inside the opening. A successful response waits for the full throw, then closes the window, acknowledges delivery, closes the laptop and enters a resting nap. A failure preserves the draft and does not enter the nap. New input or wake opens the laptop and retains the requested action through opening. Tests intercept backend requests; no test message is delivered.

## Author and export

Page navigation follows the horizontal menu order Home, Projects, Stories, Contact. A later destination pushes left; an earlier one pushes right. Detail routes use the same forward/back rule. Search-filter changes do not slide. Header and navigation remain stationary. Travel lasts about 638 ms with a 212 ms settling interval. Rapid navigation, resize, hiding the tab and reduced-motion changes cancel the previous transition and dispose its departing renderer.

The website passes normalized progress into `player.setHostTransition({phase:'depart'|'arrive',direction:-1|1,progress:0..1})` and clears it with `player.clearHostTransition()`. Direction is actual screen travel. The scene's saved `hostTransition.actors` bindings select four editable sparse clips. They blend over current behavior rather than replace its state. The outgoing page borrows its existing illustration mount, freezes its task clock, and continues the authored travel response; incoming scenes keep their behavior running. Ownership passes back on cancellation or disposes once the departed route is released, avoiding a second rig compilation. Playing embeds coalesce progress into their animation frame; paused departing embeds repaint immediately. Explicit-progress website mounts use `hostMotion:false` to avoid redundant host layout reads. Shoulder and robe contours lean and rebound over a planted lower body while wrist contacts remain stable. Home and Contact foliage pivots from the soil and rebounds after the stop; the pot stays fixed. Contact retains the paper-plane hand contact. The shared runtime blends path contours, supports paused rendering, and suppresses this overlay for reduced motion.

Studio's **Page motion** panel assigns actors and clips, previews phase/direction/progress, and saves those bindings in the scene document. Clip shape and timing remain editable in the ordinary animation editor. `test/studio-page-motion-browser.mjs` checks the panel; `test/portfolio-route-motion-browser.mjs` checks live departure motion, direction, rapid navigation, Back, mobile width and reduced motion.

The gallery exposes `wwwzard-stories`, `wwwzard-projects` and `wwwzard-contact`. Open each in Studio to edit paths, props, clips and behavior. Studio editing, JSON save/reopen and website HTML export were checked for all three. Source factories live in `examples/wwwzard-*.js`; JSON exports contain the authored result.

Run `npm run export:portfolio` to regenerate `releases/wwwzard-{stories,projects,contact}.scene.json`. An optional destination directory can be passed after `--`. Copy the exports into the website's `src/characters` directory and build the site. The existing runtime archive does not need to be republished for scene-only changes.

The portfolio's `PortfolioCharacter` adapter loads the selected scene and runtime when visible, disposes playback on route unmount, and retains the previous artwork on loading failure. Reduced motion and offscreen suspension use the shared runtime. Contact includes the same room artwork as Home in its exported scene, including the plant and window.

## Evidence and limits

`test/portfolio-scenes-browser.mjs` checks desktop and 390px layouts, input/result signals with a mocked backend, reduced motion, route cleanup and offscreen resume. `test/portfolio-contact-story-browser.mjs` checks the complete form sequence, validation and wake. `test/portfolio-interactions-browser.mjs` checks native prop clicks, nearby gusts and autonomous section actions on the built portfolio. `test/portfolio-scenes-studio.mjs` checks actual editor controls and export; `test/pointer-proximity-studio.mjs` checks radius/direction editing and save/reopen. Scene-specific tests review actions and interruptions through the public runtime. Screenshot sheets and browser reports are under `test-results/`.

The scene budget is 30 KiB gzip each. With body and plant travel clips, exported JSON measures 28,556 bytes for Home, 6,187 for Stories, 7,855 for Projects and 17,503 for Contact. Two simultaneously rendered 300px travel reactions measured 5.0 ms p95 against a 12 ms update budget on the host desktop. This excludes browser paint and is recorded in `test-results/host-transition-cost.json`. The previous quiet-playback check measured 3.6 ms for Home, 2.4 ms for Stories, 2.4 ms for Projects and 2.7 ms for Contact. The installed package adds roughly 72 kB gzip shared between scenes, in addition to scene payloads and the host site's own code.

These are 120 warm samples of controller and SVG-update CPU time. They exclude browser paint and do not establish physical-phone frame rate, startup, sustained battery cost or total memory. The scenes add no raster textures, but browser DOM and path memory have not been profiled. Physical-device review remains necessary. See `test-results/portfolio-scenes/cost.json` for the measurement details.

Actual built-page comparison on September 27, 2026 used the same six warmed routes, desktop headless Edge, 1280×900, no CPU throttling. The p95 animation-frame interval improved from 36.0 to 18.2 ms; intervals over 25 ms fell from 18/211 to 5/266 and long animation frames fell from 16 to 5. The updated scene includes the additional body and plant motion. Reusing the departing renderer, coalescing incoming renders, bounded clip/contour caches, avoiding host layout sampling, and isolating moving artwork paint account for the changed work. This is a desktop browser comparison, not a physical-phone or guaranteed 60 fps claim. Raw records: `test-results/route-performance/before.json` and `after.json`.
